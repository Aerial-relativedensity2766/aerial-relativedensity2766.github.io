## 🧭 What is Web-Pilot?

Web-Pilot is a **local-first AI browser agent** that lives on your computer. It understands plain English instructions and turns them into safe, structured browser actions. Imagine telling a smart assistant "Find me the best price for a coffee maker under $100" and watching it browse the web, visit shopping sites, filter results, and bring back the answer—all by itself.

Unlike cloud-based assistants, Web-Pilot runs **entirely on your own machine**. Your data stays with you. No accounts, no subscriptions, no cloud dependency. It's your personal, self-hosted web companion.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🗣️ Natural Language Commands** – Type instructions in everyday language. No programming needed.
- **🌐 Smart Browsing** – Uses Playwright technology to navigate websites just like you would.
- **🔍 Intelligent Extraction** – Pulls out useful information from pages automatically.
- **🧠 Semantic Filtering** – Understands meaning, not just keywords, to show you what matters.
- **📥 Safe Downloads** – Downloads files only when you permit it.
- **🔒 Local-First Privacy** – Everything runs on your device. No data leaves your computer.
- **🛡️ Safe Actions** – Structured guardrails prevent harmful or unintended browser actions.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Web-Pilot really private?

Yes! Web-Pilot runs completely on your computer. Your browsing history, searches, and downloaded files never leave your device. No cloud servers are involved.

### Do I need to know how to code?

Absolutely not. Web-Pilot was designed for everyday users. If you can type a sentence, you can use Web-Pilot.

### Can Web-Pilot visit any website?

Web-Pilot can navigate most public websites. Some sites may block automated browsing, and Web-Pilot will let you know if it encounters such a site.

### What happens if Web-Pilot makes a mistake?

Web-Pilot includes safety features that prevent harmful actions. If something seems uncertain, it will ask for your confirmation before proceeding.

### Can I stop Web-Pilot mid-task?

Yes! Just click the "Stop" button in the Web-Pilot window, and it will halt all current activities immediately.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Web-Pilot won't open
- Make sure you have a stable internet connection
- Try running the application as administrator (right-click → "Run as administrator")
- Check that Windows Firewall isn't blocking the app

### Web-Pilot seems slow
- Close other heavy applications to free up memory
- Ensure your internet connection is stable
- Try simpler commands

### Download page doesn't load
- Refresh the page
- Try a different browser
- Check your internet connection
